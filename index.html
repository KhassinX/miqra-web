The Tanakh, as it is read.

Miqra is a reverent reader for the Tanakh — the Hebrew Scriptures, presented faithfully, the original alongside a translation. Ask a question in plain language and Miqra surfaces the passages that relate to it. The concordance runs entirely on your device, and it never interprets, paraphrases, or invents a single word. It shows you the text. The reading is yours.

Coming to the App Store · Free · No ads · No tracking · Universal Apple

Miqra is built by KhassinX, an independent studio for privacy-first Apple platform apps.

The text, faithfully

Original and translation, side by side

Read the public-domain Hebrew of the Tanakh next to an English translation, in a column built for long, comfortable reading. Nothing is paraphrased; the original stays exactly as it is.

All twenty-four books

The whole Tanakh — Torah, Nevi'im, and Ketuvim. Move between books and chapters instantly, keep your place, and return to where you left off.

Transparent provenance

Miqra tells you which edition you are reading. The Hebrew is the masoretic text (Tanach with Ta'amei Hamikra, via Sefaria); the translation is public-domain, attributed plainly. No silent edits, no harmonizing of differing readings.

A concordance that finds, never authors

This is the heart of Miqra. You can ask in your own words — "passages about refuge," "where the covenant is renewed" — and Miqra brings back the verses that relate to your question. It is a concordance, not a commentary.

It surfaces connections

The search understands meaning, not just matching words, so related passages come to the surface even when they share no exact phrase. Every result is the literal text of the Tanakh — verbatim.

It never interprets

Miqra does not explain, summarize, paraphrase, or generate scripture. There is no generative model writing about the text. It points you to passages; what they mean is yours to read and to study.

It runs on your device

The concordance uses Apple's on-device natural-language model. Your questions and your reading never leave your device to reach us or any third party. It works offline.

Private by design

Nothing is collected

Miqra has no servers and no analytics. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. The App Privacy Label reads "Data Not Collected" because nothing is.

Everything stays with you

Your questions, searches, reading position, bookmarks, and notes live only on your device. No accounts, no sign-in, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

Free, as an offering

Miqra is offered pro bono — free, with no ads and no tracking. An optional tip jar lets you support the work if you wish. There is no paywall on the text.

Universal Apple

Miqra is designed for each device, not stretched to fit. Read on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch. Available in English at launch, with a Spanish translation on the way.
