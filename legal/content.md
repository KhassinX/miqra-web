# Content & Rights

Miqra presents the Tanakh in Hebrew alongside a translation, drawn from public-domain editions and shown verbatim, with each edition attributed in the app and on our [Legal](/legal/) page. We take the rights of authors, translators, and publishers seriously — and we are always glad to welcome new, freely-licensed editions.

## Requesting removal or review of content {#removal}

If you are a rights-holder, an authority, or anyone who believes that content in Miqra should be removed or reviewed — whether on copyright grounds or to comply with a legal requirement — you are welcome to write to us. We review every request in good faith and respond promptly.

Please email **[legal@khassinx.com](mailto:legal@khassinx.com)** with:

- **The specific content** — the edition, book, chapter, and passage, with enough detail for us to locate it.
- **The basis for your request** — the right you hold or the legal requirement you are asserting, and your authority to make the request.
- **How to reach you** for any follow-up.

We will acknowledge your message, review it, and reply. If content was included in error, or if we are required to remove it, we will do so. Where a request is unclear or appears mistaken, we will explain our understanding and continue to work with you in good faith.

Miqra is operated by KHASSINX LLC, a Florida limited liability company; these matters are governed by the laws of the State of Florida. This page is provided for transparency and is not legal advice.

## Contribute a translation {#contribute}

Miqra grows with the community. If you know of a Tanakh translation from Jewish sources that is in the public domain, offered under a free license such as Creative Commons, or that its rights-holder would allow us to include, we would be honored to add it and to credit the translator and publisher in full. We can only include texts that are free to share or that we have clear written permission to use — never copyrighted work without the rights-holder's authorization.

To suggest a translation, write to **[community@khassinx.com](mailto:community@khassinx.com)**. Any edition we add is credited to its translator and publisher, in the app and on this site.
