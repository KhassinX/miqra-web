One-line description

Miqra is a reverent Tanakh reader with an on-device concordance that surfaces connections — and never interprets.

One-paragraph description

Miqra is a Tanakh reader for Apple platforms. It presents the public-domain Hebrew text alongside a translation, side by side, and lets you ask in plain language to find related passages. The concordance runs entirely on your device using Apple's natural-language model; it surfaces connections but never interprets, paraphrases, or generates a single word of scripture. Everything stays on your device — no servers, no analytics, no tracking. Miqra is universal across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch, and it is offered pro bono, free of charge.

What makes it different

  • Non-generative by principle — the concordance finds; it never authors or interprets scripture
  • Faithful presentation — original and translation side by side, each edition attributed, no silent edits
  • Fully on-device — search and reading never leave your device; it works offline
  • Pro bono — free, no ads, no data-harvesting business model
